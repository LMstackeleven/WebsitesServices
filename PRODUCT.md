# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (no framework). User-chosen for a fast, host-anywhere landing page.

## Users

Primary: owners and operators of racket-sports businesses — pickleball clubs, indoor/outdoor facilities, camps, programs, tournaments, and leagues (e.g. the segment shown in the portfolio: PingPod, Dill Dinkers, Premier Pickleball Club, Gotham Pickleball). Typically time-poor operators who need a professional web presence but lack an in-house design team. They are evaluating whether to hire StackEleven to design/build their site.

## Product Purpose

Landing page selling StackEleven's **web design services** (websites and landing pages) to racket-sports businesses. Success = visitor books a call or requests a quote. It is a lead-capture / persuade surface, not the studio's whole site.

## Positioning

StackEleven is a design studio that already knows the racket-sports world — it has produced ad campaigns and marketing collateral for pickleball clubs, camps, and tournaments. The web-design offering is that same niche fluency applied to websites: a designer who understands court time, memberships, clinics, tournaments, and league sign-ups, not a generic web shop learning the vocabulary. Tagline in use: "Graphic Design that Works."

## Operating Context

Clients run facilities and programs whose web needs center on: membership and package sign-ups, class/clinic and camp registration, tournament and event promotion, court booking or scheduling links, and local discovery. The landing page must speak to those concrete jobs. Prospects likely arrive from referrals, social, or outreach and decide quickly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Service offered: web design (websites + landing pages). Scope of build vs. design-only, packages, and pricing are **undecided** — do not invent tiers or prices.
- Contact channels on hand: email info@stackeleven.com, phone +1 (480) 398 0150, site www.stackeleven.com.
- Conversion mechanism (embedded scheduler vs. contact form vs. mailto/tel) is **undecided**; default to a request-a-quote form plus visible phone/email until the user specifies a scheduler.
- Static site — any form needs a real endpoint (undecided); do not fabricate a working backend.

- Logo mark: three stacked pickleball-paddle silhouettes (ties the brand directly to racket sports).
- Assets available in `Branding (Brand guidelines, colors, typography)_/`: `icon.png` / `icon w.png` (white) / `icon b.png` (black) marks, `StackEleven Marketing.png` (full lockup, black text), `StackEleven Marketing w.png` (white, for dark backgrounds), plus `.ai` source. - No website case studies or client testimonials on hand yet — do not fabricate web-design portfolio pieces, client quotes, stats, or results. Use the graphic-design work as proof of niche + craft; label it honestly.

## Product Principles

1. Speak racket-sports operator, not generic "small business" — use their real jobs (memberships, clinics, tournaments, camps).
2. Lead with proof of niche fluency; the portfolio is the credibility engine.
3. Every section serves the one goal: book a call / request a quote.
4. Never fabricate results, testimonials, prices, or web case studies that don't exist.
5. Keep it fast and frictionless — static, quick-loading, one clear path to contact.

No product-specific standard stated. Default to solid baseline: sufficient color contrast (brand blue on white/black), keyboard-usable form, semantic structure.
